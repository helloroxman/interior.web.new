127 m²ApartmentParus Life housing complex

About the project

  • TypeApartment
  • Area127 m²
  • LocationParus Life housing complex
  • Visualisations25

A two-level apartment in a dark palette: graphite walls, warm wood and a fireplace at the heart of the living room. Red light in the bathroom, a panoramic city terrace and track lighting give the space the character of a night loft.

The gallery covers the floor plan, hallway, corridor, staircase, living room, dining area, dining room, kitchen, bathroom.
