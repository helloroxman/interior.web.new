Scandinavian coziness for a large house in Lviv: white walls, terracotta accents, black wardrobes and green textiles. Plenty of daylight, simple forms and places for slow living.

The gallery covers the floor plan, bedroom, walk-in wardrobe, bathroom, children's room, kitchen, dining area, living room, hallway.
