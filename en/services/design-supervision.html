Design supervision during renovation

Even the best project can be spoiled during renovation. Supervision by a professional designer helps deliver the interior on schedule and exactly as visualised.

Order a design project View projects

Design supervision is the second stage of work after the design project. We prepare the cost estimate, control the renovation and help select materials, so the decisions from the visualisations are not lost between the drawing board and the site.

Construction takes enormous money, effort and time when you do it all yourself. A designer's supervision reduces rework and mistakes, and so saves both money and time.

What the project includes

  • 3D interior visualisation
  • Furniture drawings
  • Construction drawings
  • Material selection

Frequently asked questions

What does design supervision include?

A cost estimate, renovation control and material selection.

Why is supervision needed if there are drawings?

Drawings describe what to do; supervision makes sure it is done exactly that way. The designer spots deviations from the project in time and helps make decisions when something on site does not go to plan.

Which properties is supervision available for?

Apartments, houses, offices and commercial spaces — every type of property the studio designs.

How much does design supervision cost?

The price depends on the property and the scope of work. Get in touch and we will calculate it for your project.
